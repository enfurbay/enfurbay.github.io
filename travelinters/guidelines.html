Community Guidelines

Version 2026-10-04

Be who you say you are

Use your own photos and your real first name. One account per person.

Be clear about what you want

The intent you pick on a trip is shown to others. Respect theirs.

Take no for an answer

A declined request or an unanswered message is an answer. Do not ask again.

Keep it public

Confirmed meeting points must be public places. Do not pressure anyone to meet somewhere private.

No selling

No advertising, recruiting, paid services or requests for money.

Report what is wrong

Reports are private. The person you report is never told who reported them.
