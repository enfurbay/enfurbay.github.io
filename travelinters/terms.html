Spotlight

Spotlight is an optional purchase, separate from Tripvo Pro, sold by Apple as consumable credits. One credit shows one of your upcoming trips first in Discover for 30 minutes, to travelers whose dates overlap yours in that city. Purchased credits do not expire. They belong to the account that bought them: they cannot be transferred, exchanged for money or moved to another account, and they end if you delete the account. Refunds are handled only by Apple. A running Spotlight ends early only if the trip or the account can no longer be shown in Discover: if the trip ends, or we restrict, suspend or ban the account, that credit is returned to your balance. If you remove the trip yourself, its Spotlight ends and counts as used. Pausing discovery or turning on incognito hides you while it runs; its 30 minutes keep running.
