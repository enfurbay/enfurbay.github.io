Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-04

What we collect

Your first name, date of birth (to confirm you are an adult and show your age), profile photos, languages and travel style; the trips, places, areas, availability and plans you enter; messages you send; reports you file; purchase status; and device diagnostics such as crash reports.

What other travelers see

Your first name, age, photos, and the parts of your trip that overlap with theirs. Never your surname, date of birth, exact accommodation, private preferences, or who else you have matched with.

Waves and passes

A wave is shown to nobody until the traveler you waved at waves at you too; then you are connected and can message each other. Passing on a traveler is never shown to anyone. Both are stored so Discover can work, and are deleted with your account.

Plans shared with a trusted person

A traveler you have a plan with can send one person they trust a private link. It shows the plan's time and place, your first name, your main photo and whether you are photo verified, and nothing else about you. Search engines cannot find it, we do not record who opens it, and it stops working a day after the plan, or at once if the plan is cancelled or sharing is stopped.

Rules for messages

When you send a message, it is checked automatically against fixed pattern rules: messages that ask for money or carry bank, card or crypto details are not sent, and phone numbers, e-mail addresses and social handles are not sent until you two have a plan together. No person and no AI reads your messages. A refused message is not stored; we keep only that a rule applied, which one and when, for 90 days.

Meetups

After a plan, each of you can say whether you met. Your answer is private and is never shown to anyone. When both say yes, each profile shows how many travelers that person has met, never who.

What we never collect

We do not track you across other apps or websites, we do not sell your data, and we do not collect your live location.

Places you've been

If you choose, you can add a list of cities you have visited, by month or year. It can be built on your device from the place and date labels of your photos: the photos themselves are never opened or uploaded, the cities are worked out on your device with nothing looked up online, and you review the list before anything is saved. Other travelers see the cities and years; travelers you are connected with also see the month. You can remove the list at any time, and it is deleted with your account.

Free now status

If you say when you are free on a trip, travelers who match that trip see it until it ends by itself, a day later at most. It says a time and, if you add one, a short note. It never says where you are.

Verification selfies

A verification selfie is taken live with the front camera. Your phone compares it with your main photo; that comparison stays on your phone and we keep no face data. The selfie itself is visible only to our reviewers, is used only to compare with your profile photos, and is deleted as soon as it is reviewed, after 30 days at the latest. We keep only the result.

Invites

If you invite a friend or use a friend's invite code, we record which account invited which so both can get the reward. Neither of you is shown who the other is. To keep rewards for new travelers, we keep a one-way fingerprint of the Apple sign-in an account used, which cannot be turned back into your Apple ID and stays after the account is deleted, and a random number for this device that is used for nothing else.

Calendar

If you add a plan to your calendar we write that one event. If you import a trip from your calendar, the events are read on your device to suggest a destination and dates; they are not uploaded.

Analytics and diagnostics

We record which features are used and when the app crashes, using a fixed list of non-sensitive fields. Message text, dates of birth and coordinates are never included.

Where data is stored

On servers in the United Kingdom operated by our infrastructure provider, and on your device for offline use.

Your choices

You can edit or delete anything you entered, pause discovery, make a trip private, and delete your account and its data from Profile → Account.
