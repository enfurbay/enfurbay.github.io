Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-04

What we collect

Your first name, date of birth (to confirm you are an adult and show your age), profile photos, languages and travel style; the trips, places, areas, availability and plans you enter; messages you send; reports you file; purchase status; and device diagnostics such as crash reports.

What other travelers see

Your first name, age, photos, and the parts of your trip that overlap with theirs. Never your surname, date of birth, exact accommodation, private preferences, or who else you have matched with.

What we never collect

We do not track you across other apps or websites, we do not sell your data, and we do not collect your live location.

Verification selfies

A selfie you submit for verification is visible only to our reviewers, is used only to compare with your profile photos, and is deleted with your account.

Calendar

If you add a plan to your calendar we write that one event. If you import a trip from your calendar, the events are read on your device to suggest a destination and dates; they are not uploaded.

Analytics and diagnostics

We record which features are used and when the app crashes, using a fixed list of non-sensitive fields. Message text, dates of birth and coordinates are never included.

Where data is stored

On servers in the United Kingdom operated by our infrastructure provider, and on your device for offline use.

Your choices

You can edit or delete anything you entered, pause discovery, make a trip private, and delete your account and its data from Profile → Account.

Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@travelinters.app
